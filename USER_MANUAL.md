## Getting Started

### Welcome to AI Tax Lawyer
AI Tax Lawyer is your personal AI-powered tax advisor designed specifically for Bangladeshi taxpayers. Our platform helps you legally optimize your taxes using official NBR (National Board of Revenue) rules and regulations.

### What You Can Do
- Get instant answers to tax questions
- Calculate your tax liability accurately
- Find tax deductions you might have missed
- Generate professional tax reports
- Plan your taxes for multiple years
- Access 24/7 tax guidance

### System Requirements
- **Internet Connection**: Stable internet for real-time features
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Mobile**: Works on all smartphones and tablets

## Using the Chat Feature

### Starting a Conversation

1. **Access Chat**
   - Click "Start Consultation" from homepage
   - Or click "Ask Tax Question" from dashboard
   - Or use the navigation menu "Chat"

2. **Chat Interface Overview**
   - **Header**: Shows your taxpayer type
   - **Message Area**: Displays conversation history
   - **Input Box**: Where you type your questions
   - **Quick Actions**: Pre-written common questions

### Asking Questions

**Best Practices for Questions**
1. **Be Specific**: "How much can I deduct for home office expenses?" instead of "Tell me about deductions"
2. **Provide Context**: Mention your income level, taxpayer type, or specific situation
3. **One Topic at a Time**: Ask about one tax topic per message for clearer answers

**Example Questions**
- "I'm a salaried employee earning 8 lakh BDT annually. What deductions can I claim?"
- "How do I calculate tax on freelance income from foreign clients?"
- "What's the current tax rate for rental income in Bangladesh?"
- "Can I claim medical expenses as a tax deduction?"

### Understanding AI Responses

**Response Features**
- **Main Answer**: Clear explanation of your tax question
- **Sources**: References to NBR documents and regulations
- **Confidence Score**: How confident the AI is in the answer
- **Related Topics**: Suggestions for follow-up questions

**Reading Citations**
- Look for numbered references [1], [2], etc.
- Sources are listed at the bottom of responses
- Click on source titles to learn more

### Quick Actions

Use pre-written questions for common topics:
- "What deductions can I claim?"
- "Calculate my tax liability"
- "What's my tax rate?"
- "How do I file my return?"

### Chat Tips

**Do:**
- Ask follow-up questions for clarification
- Mention specific amounts and situations
- Save important conversations
- Use the language you're comfortable with

**Don't:**
- Share sensitive personal information like TIN numbers
- Ask for illegal tax evasion advice
- Expect 100% accuracy for complex situations
- Use the chat for non-tax related questions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Login Problems**

*Issue*: Can't log in to account
*Solutions*:
1. Check email/password spelling
2. Use "Forgot Password" link
3. Try different browser
4. Clear browser cache and cookies
5. Contact support if using social login

*Issue*: Account locked or suspended
*Solutions*:
1. Check email for notifications
2. Wait 30 minutes and try again
3. Contact customer support
4. Verify account through email link

**Chat Issues**

*Issue*: AI not responding or slow responses
*Solutions*:
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try shorter, simpler questions
4. Clear browser cache
5. Switch to different browser

*Issue*: Incorrect or irrelevant answers
*Solutions*:
1. Provide more specific context
2. Rephrase your question
3. Break complex questions into parts
4. Mention your taxpayer type and income level
5. Use the feedback option to report issues

**Calculator Problems**

*Issue*: Calculation seems wrong
*Solutions*:
1. Double-check input values
2. Ensure correct taxpayer type selected
3. Verify current tax year
4. Compare with NBR tax tables
5. Use the chat feature for clarification

*Issue*: Can't save calculation results
*Solutions*:
1. Check if logged in properly
2. Ensure stable internet connection
3. Try calculating again
4. Contact support for data recovery

**Payment Issues**

*Issue*: Payment failed or not processed
*Solutions*:
1. Check payment method balance
2. Verify card/mobile money details
3. Try different payment method
4. Contact your bank/mobile operator
5. Reach out to our billing support

*Issue*: Subscription not activated after payment
*Solutions*:
1. Wait 5-10 minutes for processing
2. Check email for confirmation
3. Refresh your dashboard
4. Log out and log back in
5. Contact billing support with payment reference

### Browser-Specific Issues

**Chrome Users**
- Clear cache: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
- Disable extensions that might interfere
- Update to latest Chrome version

**Firefox Users**
- Clear cache: Options → Privacy → Clear Data
- Check Enhanced Tracking Protection settings
- Update to latest Firefox version

**Safari Users**
- Clear cache: Safari → Clear History
- Check Intelligent Tracking Prevention
- Update Safari and macOS

**Mobile Users**
- Use landscape mode for better experience
- Ensure mobile data/WiFi is stable
- Close other apps to free memory
- Update mobile browser

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is AI Tax Lawyer legally authorized to provide tax advice?**
A: AI Tax Lawyer provides general tax guidance based on NBR rules and regulations. While our advice is based on official tax laws, we recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional for complex situations or official filing.

**Q: How accurate is the tax advice?**
A: Our AI is trained on official NBR documents and regulations with regular updates. However, tax laws can be complex and situation-specific. We provide confidence scores with our advice and recommend professional consultation for high-stakes decisions.

**Q: Can I use this for my business taxes?**
A: Yes! We support business owners and provide guidance on corporate taxes, VAT, business deductions, and more. The Business plan includes advanced features specifically for companies.

**Q: Is my data secure and private?**
A: Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption, secure servers, and strict privacy policies. Your personal and financial information is never shared with third parties.

### Features and Usage

**Q: What's the difference between the chat and calculator?**
A: The chat provides conversational advice and answers specific questions. The calculator gives precise tax liability calculations based on your income and deductions. Both work together to give you comprehensive tax guidance.

**Q: Can I get advice in Bengali?**
A: Yes! Our platform supports both English and Bengali. You can switch languages in your profile settings and ask questions in either language.

**Q: How often are tax laws updated in the system?**
A: We monitor NBR announcements and update our system within 24-48 hours of any new regulations or SRO publications.

**Q: Can I upload my tax documents for analysis?**
A: Document upload is available for Pro and Business subscribers. You can upload salary certificates, investment statements, and other tax documents for personalized analysis.

### Billing and Subscriptions

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes, you can cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of your billing period, and you won't be charged for the next cycle.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for annual subscriptions. Contact support within 30 days if you're not satisfied.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept bKash, Nagad, and credit/debit cards through SSL Commerce. Payment methods are specifically chosen for convenience in Bangladesh.

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
A: Yes! Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

### Technical Support

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: We support Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, use the latest version of your preferred browser.

**Q: Can I use this on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works great on smartphones and tablets.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You'll receive an email with instructions to reset your password.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: No, AI Tax Lawyer requires an internet connection for real-time tax advice and calculations. However, you can download reports for offline reference.

### Tax-Specific Questions

**Q: Can you help with previous years' taxes?**
A: Yes! You can ask about previous tax years, though our primary focus is on current tax year guidance. Historical tax rate information is available through our chat feature.

**Q: Do you cover all types of income?**
A: We cover salary, business income, rental income, freelance income, foreign remittances, capital gains, and investment income as per Bangladesh tax laws.

**Q: Can you help with VAT and other taxes?**
A: Yes, our Business plan includes guidance on VAT, customs duty, and other business-related taxes in Bangladesh.

**Q: What if my situation is very complex?**
A: For highly complex situations involving multiple income sources, large businesses, or unique circumstances, we recommend consulting with a qualified Chartered Accountant in addition to using our platform.

### Getting Started

**Q: Do I need to know anything about taxes to use this?**
A: Not at all! AI Tax Lawyer is designed for taxpayers with any level of tax knowledge. We explain concepts in simple terms and provide step-by-step guidance.

**Q: How long does it take to set up an account?**
A: Account setup takes about 5-10 minutes, including the onboarding process to understand your tax situation.

**Q: Can I try it before subscribing?**
A: Yes! The free plan lets you ask 5 questions per month and use the basic calculator. This gives you a good feel for the platform before upgrading.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my free plan limits?**
A: If you exceed your monthly question limit on the free plan, you'll be prompted to upgrade to Pro or wait until the next month. Your existing data and conversations are always preserved.
