#### **Step 1: Know Your Tax Obligation**
- **Tax-free threshold** for FY 2024-25:
  - **Men**: BDT 450,000
  - **Women**: BDT 500,000
  - **Third-gender/65+ years**: BDT 550,000.
- If your **annual gross income exceeds the threshold**, you must file a tax return.

#### **Step 2: Get Your TIN (Tax Identification Number)**
- **Apply online** via the NBR portal ([nbr.gov.bd](https://nbr.gov.bd)):
  - Fill out the form with personal details.
  - Receive your **e-TIN certificate** instantly.

#### **Step 3: Collect Required Documents**
- **Salary slips** (showing TDS/Advance Income Tax deducted).
- **Bank statements** and **investment proofs** (e.g., DPS, life insurance).
- **Property or rental income details** (if applicable).
- **Form IT-11GA** (for salaried individuals).

#### **Step 4: Calculate Taxable Income**
- **Gross salary** includes:
  - Basic salary, allowances (HRA, conveyance, medical), bonuses, leave encashment.
- **Deduct exemptions**:
  - **Tax-free income** (threshold per gender/age).
  - **Investments**: Up to **25% of total income** (max BDT 1.5 million) eligible for **15% rebate**.

#### **Step 5: Compute Tax Liability**
- **Tax slabs for FY 2024-25**:
  - 0% for first BDT 350,000.
  - 5% for next BDT 100,000.
  - 10% for next BDT 400,000 (up to BDT 850,000).
  - 15% for income above BDT 850,000.
- **Example**: For BDT 600,000 annual salary:
  - Taxable income = BDT 600,000 – BDT 350,000 = BDT 250,000.
  - Tax = 5% of BDT 100,000 + 10% of BDT 150,000 = **BDT 20,000**.

#### **Step 6: File Tax Return Online**
- **Log in** to [NBR e-Filing portal](https://nbr.gov.bd) with e-TIN.
- **Fill Form IT-11GA**:
  - Enter salary details, deductions, and TDS credits.
- **Upload documents**: Salary certificates, bank statements, investment proofs.

#### **Step 7: Pay Remaining Tax (if any)**
- **Options**:
  - **Online banking** (e.g., bKash, Nagad, debit/credit cards).
  - **Challan payment** via authorized banks.
- **Retain payment receipt** for records.

#### **Step 8: Submit Return and Acknowledgment**
- **Deadline**: **November 30** each year.
- **Penalty for late filing**: Fines or legal action.
- **Receive acknowledgment** with a unique submission reference number.

#### **Employer’s Role (TDS Compliance)**
- **Deduct TDS monthly** from salary based on slabs.
- **Issue Form 16** (salary certificate) at year-end for return filing.

#### **Key Tips**
- **Check TDS in salary slips** to avoid double payment.
- **Use NBR’s tax calculator** for accuracy.
- **Consult a tax advisor** for complex cases (e.g., multiple income sources).

Kimi: How the Bangladesh government spots a **non-filing / non-paying taxpayer** can be summarised in **seven practical “radars”** that NBR and its field offices (DCTs) use every day.

---

### 1. **TIN & Third-Party Data Matching**
- **TIN is mandatory** for almost any economic activity—bank account opening, LC registration, vehicle registration, utility connections, trade licence, membership of professional bodies, etc.  
- Banks, mobile-financial-service providers, insurance companies and even ride-sharing apps **report interest, commissions, turnover and driver earnings electronically against each customer’s TIN**.  
- Any mismatch (e.g., bank interest > tax-free threshold but **no return filed**) triggers an **automatic discrepancy notice** from the system .

---

### 2. **Withholding-Tax (WHT) & Advance-Tax Trail**
- Every time a business **deducts WHT** on rent, professional fees, supply contracts, or an importer pays **advance income tax (AIT)** at customs, the payment is logged against the **beneficiary’s TIN**.  
- If the **aggregate WHT/AIT exceeds the tax-free limit** yet no return is submitted, the **Withholding Tax Reconciliation Report** flags the person for a “non-filer notice” .

---

### 3. **VAT Registration & BIN Cross-Checks**
- **Business Identification Number (BIN)** is compulsory for any entity crossing Tk 5 million turnover.  
- Monthly VAT returns show **sales turnover**; NBR’s **VAT Gap model** compares declared turnover with income-tax returns—**large turnover but zero or minuscule income reported** is a red flag .

---

### 4. **Physical & Desk Audits**
- NBR runs **risk-based selection algorithms** that pick returns (or non-filers) for audit.  
- Post-filing **audit steps**:  
  1. Team appointment within 7 days  
  2. 60-day field visit/investigation  
  3. Draft audit report → taxpayer explanation → final report within 300 days  
  4. If undeclared income is found, a **tax-evasion assessment order** is issued .

---

### 5. **Best-Judgment & Ex-Parte Assessment**
- If a person **never files**, or fails to respond to a notice, the Deputy Commissioner of Taxes (DCT) can pass a **best-judgment assessment** using:
  - lifestyle indicators (property, vehicles, foreign travel),
  - utility bills,
  - bank deposits, and
  - industry benchmarks .

---

### 6. **Mandatory Proof of Return (PSR) Gateways**
- **PSR (Proof of Submission of Return)** is now required for:
  - renewal of trade licence,
  - tender participation,
  - credit facilities > Tk 500,000,
  - membership of professional bodies,
  - registration of vehicles above 1500 cc.  
- Any application without a valid PSR is **automatically rejected**, forcing the person into the tax net .

---

### 7. **Exit & High-Value Transaction Monitoring**
- **Leaving Bangladesh** (work visa expiry, immigration record) or **transfer of immovable property** triggers an **automatic check** of return status.  
- **Non-resident asset sales** or **large cash deposits** (CTR > Tk 1 million in a day) are reported by banks to Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit, which forwards non-filer cases to NBR.

---

### In Short
The government does **not rely on chance**; it uses **data convergence**—banks, customs, utilities, professional bodies, immigration, and digital platforms all feed into NBR’s central server. If you are economically active but not in the tax rolls, **the probability of staying undetected is shrinking every quarter**.
