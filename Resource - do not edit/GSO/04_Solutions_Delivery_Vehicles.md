# Solutions Delivery Vehicles ("The How")

## Delivery Methods Framework

### Personal Attention Levels:

- **One-on-One**: Individual, personalized service
- **Small Group**: 5-15 people, interactive sessions
- **One-to-Many**: Unlimited scalability, mass delivery

### Effort Levels:

- **Done-For-You (DFY)**: We do everything for them
- **Done-With-You (DWY)**: We guide them through the process
- **Do-It-Yourself (DIY)**: They do it with our tools/resources

### Delivery Mediums:

- **Live**: Real-time, interactive sessions
- **Recorded**: Pre-made content they can access anytime
- **Written**: Guides, checklists, templates
- **Chat/AI**: Interactive conversational support
