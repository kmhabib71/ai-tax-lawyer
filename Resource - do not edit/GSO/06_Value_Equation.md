# Value Equation for Tax Offer

## The Hormozi Value Equation Formula

```
VALUE = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)
```

### Goal: Maximize the Top, Minimize the Bottom

**Top Side (INCREASE):**

- Dream Outcome - What they want to achieve
- Perceived Likelihood of Achievement - How likely they believe success is

**Bottom Side (DECREASE):**

- Time Delay - How long until they get results
- Effort and Sacrifice - How much work they must do
