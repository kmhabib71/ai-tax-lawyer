Here's a complete breakdown of the major tax return forms and situational filing types:

🗂️ Main Types of Income Tax Return Files in Bangladesh (As per NBR)
Form Name	Purpose	Who Should File It
IT-11GA	Personal income tax return	🔹 Salaried individuals
🔹 Freelancers
🔹 Sole proprietors (small business without company registration)
IT-11KA	Company or Firm income tax return	🔹 Limited companies (Private/Public Ltd.)
🔹 Partnerships
🔹 Branch offices of foreign companies
IT-12BA	Return for businesspersons subject to presumptive tax	🔹 Small traders
🔹 Business not maintaining full accounts
🔹 Under sections like 82BB (minimum tax)
IT-10B	Statement of assets, liabilities, and expenses	🔹 Mandatory for individual taxpayers above exemption limit or with certain assets
IT-10BB	Wealth statement for business taxpayers	🔹 Business owners with turnover beyond threshold
IT-10A	Statement of TDS deducted and deposited	🔹 Companies or employers deducting TDS (rent, salary, contractors)
IT-10E	Statement of tax rebate claim	🔹 Individual claiming rebate on investment, donation, etc.
IT-10J	Advance tax payment statement	🔹 Taxpayers paying advance tax (companies, large businesses)

📄 Other Related Filing Documents
Name	Use Case
TIN Registration Form	First-time taxpayer (get eTIN)
TIN Amendment Form	If there's a change in name, address, source of income
SRO Acknowledgement Forms	For businesses seeking exemption or preferential treatment under special rules
VAT Registration Form (Mushak 1.1)	For businesses with turnover above threshold or trading in VAT-applicable goods
Mushak 6.3, 6.6, 6.7	Monthly VAT return forms
Employee Salary Statement (Form 24)	Companies listing all salaries paid and TDS deducted
Annual TDS Return (108)	For reporting all TDS deducted and submitted by an entity annually

🧾 Summary by Situation
Situation	Return Form(s)
🔹 Salaried employee	IT-11GA, IT-10E, IT-10B
🔹 Freelancer / Sole business	IT-11GA, IT-10B, IT-10E
🔹 Small trader (without books)	IT-12BA
🔹 Landlord (rental income)	IT-11GA or IT-12BA + IT-10B
🔹 Registered company (LTD)	IT-11KA, IT-10A, IT-10BB, IT-10J
🔹 Partnership firm	IT-11KA, IT-10A, IT-10BB
🔹 Business with staff	Employee Salary Statement + TDS + IT-10A
🔹 Export/import firm	IT-11KA + VAT Forms + TDS + wealth statement

🔹 Advance tax payer	IT-10J (quarterly/half-yearly reporting)
