Customs Act, 1969 (Act No. IV of 1969) এর সংশোধন

-| Act No. IV of 1969 এর section 119B এর সন্নিবেশ।- Customs Act,
1969 (Act No. IV of 1969), অতঃপর উক্ত Act বলিয়া উল্লিখিত, এর section 119A
এর পর নিম্নরুপ নৃতন section 119B সন্নিবেশিত হইবে, যথা:-

“119B. Performing the operation of bonded warehouse
electronically.- Notwithstanding anything contained in any other
provisions of this Act or in any other law for the time being in force, the
Board may, by notification in the official Gazette, declare that the
operation of any bonded warehouse may be performed electronically
subject to such conditions, restrictions and procedures as specified in that
notification.” |
